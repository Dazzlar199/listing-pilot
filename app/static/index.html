What the agent does
  1. Spark separates the product from props and lists what it can't know.
  2. SAM 3.1 cuts the product out; pixels are placed on pure white at 85%+ fill.
  3. Spark inspects the result. If it fails, Muse Image re-shoots it in a studio and the loop runs again.
  4. Spark writes the copy; a rule engine checks Amazon's 2026 rules and sends back only what failed.
  5. Muse Image stages lifestyle shots; Spark checks the product still matches.
